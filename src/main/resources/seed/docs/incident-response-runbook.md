# Incident Response Runbook

Confidential engineering runbook. Synthetic demo content.

## Severity 1 Incidents

For a Severity 1 incident the on-call engineer must acknowledge the page within 5 minutes and appoint an incident commander. The status page must be updated every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved.

## Escalation

If the on-call engineer does not acknowledge within 5 minutes, the page escalates automatically to the secondary on-call and then to the engineering manager.

## Postmortems

A blameless postmortem must be published within 5 business days of every Severity 1 or Severity 2 incident.
