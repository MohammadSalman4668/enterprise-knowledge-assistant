# Travel and Expense Policy

Effective from 1 January 2026. This document is synthetic demo content.

## Hotel Accommodation

Hotel accommodation is capped at $200 per night in all cities. Bookings above the cap require written pre-approval from a director.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance while travelling is $60. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Submitting Expenses

Expense claims must be submitted in the finance system within 30 days of the expense date. Itemised receipts are required for every expense over $25. Late claims are rejected unless the finance team grants an exception.
