# Business Travel FAQ

Frequently asked questions about business travel. Synthetic demo content.

## How do I book travel?

All flights and hotels must be booked through the TravelDesk portal. Flights shorter than 6 hours must be booked in economy class.

## What is the hotel limit?

The hotel cap is $180 per night. Choose hotels from the preferred supplier list where possible.

## Do I need travel insurance?

Company travel insurance covers all approved business trips automatically. Download the insurance card from the TravelDesk portal before you travel.
