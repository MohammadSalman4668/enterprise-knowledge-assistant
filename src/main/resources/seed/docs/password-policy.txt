PASSWORD AND MFA STANDARD

This standard applies to every corporate account. Synthetic demo content.

PASSWORD LENGTH

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of several random words are recommended.

MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN and all cloud applications. Use the Authenticator app as the primary second factor.

PASSWORD CHANGES

Passwords are not rotated on a fixed schedule. You must change your password immediately if you suspect it has been exposed.
