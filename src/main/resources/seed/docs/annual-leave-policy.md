# Annual Leave Policy

This policy applies to all permanent employees of Northwind Synthetic Ltd. All names and figures in this document are synthetic.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata allowance based on their contracted hours. Public holidays are granted in addition to the annual leave entitlement.

## Requesting Leave

Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days before the first day of leave. Requests of more than 10 consecutive working days require approval from both the line manager and the department head.

## Carry-over

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
