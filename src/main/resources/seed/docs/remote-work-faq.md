# Remote Work FAQ

Answers to common questions about hybrid and remote work. Synthetic demo content.

## How many days can I work remotely?

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's agreement. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time.

## Is there an equipment allowance?

New remote workers receive a one-time home office equipment stipend of $500.
